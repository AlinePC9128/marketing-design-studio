04 / DESIGN PROCESS

From brief to production-ready system.

A repeatable process helps me move quickly without losing intention, consistency or quality control.

01

Understand

Read the brief, audience, business goal, channel and constraints. Identify what must be understood first.

02

Structure

Organize information, define hierarchy, map the user flow and choose the right format before styling.

03

Explore

Build visual directions through references, typography, color, layout and focused design alternatives.

04

Produce

Create the system, component variations, responsive adaptations and export-ready assets.

05

Review and learn

Check accessibility basics, technical specifications, brand consistency and performance signals.
